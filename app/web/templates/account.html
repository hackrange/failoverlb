How to move your two factor codes to a different phone, and what to do when the old one has gone.

Getting a new phone

Do this while you still have the old one. Put in your password and a code from the current app, scan the new QR with the new phone, then type a code from it to confirm. Nothing changes until that last step. If you scan the code and then get distracted, your old app keeps working, which is the right way round.

The old phone is already gone

Use a recovery code where it asks for a code from your app. That is what they are for. Each one works once. No recovery codes left either? Any admin can clear your authenticator from the Users page, and you set up a fresh one the next time you sign in.

Why it asks for your password again

Because a signed in browser on its own is not proof it is still you. Without asking, anybody who got hold of your session could swap the second factor for one they hold, and you would be locked out of your own account with nothing looking wrong.

What happens to your other sessions

Changing your authenticator signs you out everywhere else. People usually do this either because they have a new phone or because they are worried somebody else is in the account, and in the second case leaving the other session alive would defeat the point.
