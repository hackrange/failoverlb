Backup

Take the whole configuration off this fleet as one encrypted file, and put it back on this fleet or on a new one.
A backup holds every private key this fleet has, which is enough on its own to serve every site it serves. It is encrypted with AES-256 and the password is your own, so keep it somewhere you would keep the keys.

What travels is the configuration: sites, pools, zones, TCP and UDP, error pages, certificates, paid authorities, settings and accounts. What stays behind is anything about these particular machines, because that belongs to them and not to the configuration.

Take a backup

This encrypts the file, and it is the password you will need to open it again. Only a hash of it is kept here, so it cannot be filled in for you.

Restore a backup

Restoring replaces the configuration on every node in this fleet, and pushes the result out to all of them. Take a backup of what is here first if you might want it back.
